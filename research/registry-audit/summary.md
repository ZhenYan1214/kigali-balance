## T3. Same facility, same month, more than one registry or programme

A month counts when credited destruction windows at the same facility, taken from public issuance records, fall in that month for projects of at least two registries (or two programmes). ACR publishes only the vintage start date; for ACR projects without a matching ARB reporting period the window is that single month. Windows longer than 12 months are truncated to 12. This measures exposure (two ledgers drawing on one facility in one month), not double counting.
